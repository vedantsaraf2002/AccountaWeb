The apps

Three apps, one set of books

Each app is built for a different job in the business. All three read the same data your Accounta desktop software already keeps, so nothing is entered twice.

Accounta TakadaField collection

Collect payments on the doorstep, even with no signal

Your agents raise receipts where the money changes hands. Everything is stored on the phone first and pushed to your books the moment a connection comes back.

  • Works fully offline. Receipts save to the phone and sync in the background when the network returns.
  • Every receipt is location tagged, so you can see where a collection was actually made.
  • Photograph the cheque at the counter and it travels with the receipt.
  • Print on the spot to a Bluetooth receipt printer, or share a PDF.
  • Admin view for debtors position, agent-wise collection and ledgers.

Accounta TradingTrading firms

The whole trading desk, in your pocket

Check what is outstanding, what has been dispatched and what a party owes without walking back to the computer. Built for the people who are asked these questions all day.

  • Outstanding goods list with filters, and party-wise outstanding at a glance.
  • Payment dispatch report as a zoomable table you can actually read on a phone.
  • Find any bill by number or party, and send bills out in bulk as PDFs.
  • Price list and address book on hand while you are standing with a customer.
  • Sign in with an OTP on your own number — no shared passwords.

Accounta AgencyAgency operations

For agents working between the mill and the market

The same operational set, fitted to how an agency runs: orders against principals, dispatch against those orders, and what every party still owes at the end of it.

  • Indent entry from wherever you take the order.
  • Outstanding goods and party outstanding, filtered the way an agency asks for it.
  • Account ledgers for any party, without calling the office.
  • Pulls straight from your existing books — no separate data entry to maintain.
  • Search tracking, so you can pick up where you left off.
